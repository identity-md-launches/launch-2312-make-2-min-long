## Media details

- Runtime: 2:00 (120 seconds)
- Frame size: 854 × 480, 16:9 landscape, 30 fps

- Audio: AAC-LC, stereo, 48 kHz
- File size: approximately 13 MiB

## Edit notes

The edit uses the on-camera speaker and original narration from [Project Light's reference video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zosuLiiw46Y). It condenses the opening question, the call to examine inherited beliefs, and the sequence from Vedic descriptions through the Puranas and yogic interpretation. Relevant cutaway imagery already present in the reference is retained between the speaker shots. The Truth Tribe treatment is a small parchment and vermilion corner mark, with an indigo opening title.

## Limitations

The reference is 480p, so the output remains 854 × 480. The narration is sped up by about 8% to fit the two-minute runtime. This is a condensed edit, not a line-by-line reading of the full supplied script; some supporting examples and phrasing are omitted. Captions are not burned into the video.
